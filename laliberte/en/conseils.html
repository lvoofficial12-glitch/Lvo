Tree tips

Your trees, season by season.

What to keep an eye on through the year to avoid bad surprises.

SpringSummerAutumnWinter

Walk around after the thaw

  • Spot branches broken by ice
  • Check for split or leaning trunks
  • Plan pruning before the leaves come out

Keep trees healthy

  • Water young trees during heat waves
  • Watch for leaves yellowing too early
  • Clear branches from the roof and pool

Get ready for winter

  • Keep branches away from wires and the roof
  • Remove dead trees before the snow
  • Clear leaves and dead wood

Watch the snow and ice

  • Don’t shake an ice-loaded branch
  • Watch conifers bending under snow
  • Call if a branch threatens the house
