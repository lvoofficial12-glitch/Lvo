FAQ

Your questions.

How long does a head spa last?

Plan for about an hour. The exact length of each treatment will be confirmed by the salon.

Should I arrive with clean hair?

No, cleansing is part of the treatment. Just arrive a few minutes early to settle in.

Is a head spa suitable for every hair type?

Yes. The diagnosis at the start lets us adapt the products to your hair and scalp.

What’s the difference with hair botox?

A head spa cares for the scalp and relaxation. Hair botox repairs the hair fibre. The two can be combined.

How do I give a gift card?

Write to us at harmonie.sensoriel@hotmail.com and we will prepare your gift card.

Where can I park?

Parking details will be confirmed by the salon.
