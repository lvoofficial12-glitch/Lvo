THE EXPERIENCE

The ritual, step by step.

Here is what awaits you during your head spa, from the first touch to the blow-dry.

01/ 05

Diagnosis

We look at your scalp and hair and talk about your needs to tailor the treatment.

Cleansing

A gentle, warm-water cleanse that frees the scalp of build-up.

Massage

A slow massage of the head, neck and shoulders to release tension.

Steam and care

Steam opens the pores while a nourishing treatment soaks in.

Finish

Rinse, detangle and blow-dry: you leave feeling light, with soft hair.

BENEFITS

What a head spa does for you.

Less stress

The slow massage helps the body release built-up tension.

Healthy scalp

A deep cleanse for a clean, soothed scalp.

Circulation

The massage stimulates circulation in the scalp.

Shiny hair

Nourishing care leaves hair soft and glowing.

A head spa is a wellness treatment and does not replace medical advice.
